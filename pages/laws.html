PHILIPPINE LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Know the laws. Know your rights.

The Philippines has laws designed to promote women's rights, protect people from gender-based harassment, and support equality and participation.

01
RA 9710

Magna Carta of Women

A comprehensive women's human rights law that promotes women's empowerment, equality, and protection against discrimination.

02
RA 11313

Safe Spaces Act

Addresses gender-based sexual harassment in streets, public spaces, online spaces, workplaces, and educational institutions.

03
RA 7877

Anti-Sexual Harassment Act

Declares sexual harassment unlawful in employment, education, and training environments.

04
RA 11210

Expanded Maternity Leave Law

Provides maternity leave benefits and protections for covered female workers.

YOUR RIGHTS MATTER

Equality is everyone's responsibility.

Laws provide legal protection, but creating an equal and respectful environment also requires awareness and responsible action.

⚖

Right to Equality

Women have the right to equal treatment and opportunities.

🛡

Right to Protection

Women should be protected from violence, harassment, and discrimination.

🎓

Right to Education

Women and girls should have access to education and development opportunities.

🗣

Right to Participation

Women have the right to participate in decision-making and public life.
