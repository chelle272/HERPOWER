ROLE MODELS

Different paths.
One powerful message.

Filipino women have made significant contributions in medicine, journalism, sports, science, technology, leadership, and other fields.

SCIENCE & MEDICINE

Dr. Fe del Mundo

A pioneering Filipino pediatrician whose work helped shape children's healthcare in the Philippines.

MEDIA & JOURNALISM

Maria Ressa

A Filipino journalist and co-founder of Rappler recognized internationally for defending freedom of expression.

SPORTS

Hidilyn Diaz

A Filipino weightlifter who became the country's first Olympic gold medalist.

SCIENCE & INNOVATION

Aisa Mijeno

An engineer and social entrepreneur known for developing sustainable lighting solutions.

LEADERSHIP

Corazon Aquino

The first woman president of the Philippines, whose leadership became an important part of Philippine democratic history.

ARTS & PERFORMANCE

Lea Salonga

An internationally recognized Filipino performer who has represented Filipino talent on Broadway and beyond.
