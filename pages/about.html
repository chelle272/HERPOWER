ABOUT HERPOWER

Understanding Women Empowerment

Empowerment begins when women have the rights, opportunities, resources, and confidence to participate meaningfully in society.

WHAT IT MEANS

Empowerment is opportunity.

Women empowerment is the process of creating conditions that enable women to exercise their rights, access opportunities and resources, participate in decision-making, and contribute to the development of their communities and society.

In the Philippines, Republic Act No. 9710, also known as the Magna Carta of Women, recognizes women empowerment as involving the availability and accessibility of opportunities, services, and human rights that allow women to actively participate in national development.

Source: Philippine Commission on Women — Republic Act No. 9710, Magna Carta of Women.

THE PHILIPPINE CONTEXT

Women's rights are human rights.

The Philippines has laws and policies designed to protect women's rights and promote gender equality. One of the country's major laws is Republic Act No. 9710, the Magna Carta of Women.

The law recognizes the role of women in nation building and promotes substantive equality, equal opportunities, and equal access to resources and development outcomes.
